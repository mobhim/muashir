# Surface brief: site/index.html

Scope: the single-page Muashir site. It replaced the earlier wall-calendar build, which the owner rejected for its look, lack of motion, thin feature set and long dry copy.

Audience and job: individual Saudi investors and recruiters, equal weight. They should get the answer in seconds, then play with the data: rank companies, compare two, guess a reaction, open any company.

Pinned by the owner: the level of their Cerebra and Sila sites (moving background behind the whole site, glass panels, word-by-word headings, rolling numbers, a sticky stage that changes with scroll, reels and marquees), black and gold, short copy, more features.

Constraints: Arabic RTL, static files, every figure from the pipeline, not investment advice.

Unresolved: hosting/domain; real company logos (none in the project).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A night-market screen that answers one question and then hands the visitor the controls. It refuses the static report the previous build was.

OWN-WORLD: Black ground with drifting gold price lines on a fixed canvas, cream text, glass panels with a pointer-following gold spotlight, gold pills. Alexandria for headings and numbers, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for text. Green and red only for up and down.

STORY: Good results are priced in three days; after that the announcement adds nothing new; now look up your own company.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-screen opening on the moving ground. Floating glass nav pill at the top. Centre: a small gold-tagged pill with the sample size, the question at up to 6rem entering word by word, one line under it, a gold button and a glass button. Two vertical reels of company tiles run at the left and right edges in opposite directions. A scroll hint at the bottom edge.

FORM: Pinned by the owner (their own sites as the reference), not rolled. seed: none (unavailable)

Signature interaction: the sticky stage in the story, whose glass panel switches between a filling gauge, growing bars and drawing lines as each step is read. Motion grammar: one ease, blur-in words, odometers, drawn charts, rows that glide when re-sorted.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
